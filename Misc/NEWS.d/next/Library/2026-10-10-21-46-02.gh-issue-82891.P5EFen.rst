Add :class:`!_io._nibbler.TextIOWrapper`, which :func:`open`, and with
``_PYTHON_IO_DEFAULT_IMPL=_nibbler`` the :mod:`io` classes and the standard
streams, use along with the :mod:`!_io._nibbler` buffered classes. It keeps
no bytes of its own over them: a read takes from the buffer only the bytes
of the text it returns, so the buffer's position is the text position. A
:meth:`!write` or :meth:`!truncate` after a read acts at the text position,
as after ``seek(tell())``; :meth:`!tell` works while iterating over the file
(:gh:`81217`); text written goes straight into the buffer, so text and
``buffer.write()`` output keep their order (:gh:`52203`); and reads make raw
reads of the buffer size (:gh:`153656`). It also has the fixes made to the
:mod:`!_pyio` :class:`!TextIOWrapper`: reads that would block raise
:exc:`BlockingIOError` and keep a partial line, :meth:`!truncate` cuts at
the text position (:gh:`70346`), :meth:`!seek` rejects cookies :meth:`!tell`
can't have returned (:gh:`91112`), and the encoder gets ``final=True`` on
close (:gh:`79792`).
