The buffered writers of :mod:`io` now raise :exc:`OSError` when the raw
stream's :meth:`~io.RawIOBase.write` returns ``0`` for a non-empty write,
instead of retrying forever, for example in :meth:`~io.BufferedWriter.flush`
and :meth:`!close` over the Windows console. The data is kept buffered, as
for other errors.
