The buffered writers of :mod:`io` now raise :exc:`OSError` when the raw
stream's :meth:`~io.RawIOBase.write` returns ``0`` for a non-empty write,
instead of retrying forever in :meth:`~io.BufferedWriter.write`,
:meth:`~io.BufferedWriter.flush` and :meth:`!close`. This is a safety net in
the buffered layer for any raw stream that makes no progress, such as the
Windows console given an incomplete UTF-8 sequence; the raw streams
themselves are unchanged. The data is kept buffered, as for other errors.
