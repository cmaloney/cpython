When the flush done by :meth:`!close` of a buffered binary stream of
:mod:`io` fails, the stream is now closed completely, raw stream included,
and the data still buffered is discarded with a :exc:`RuntimeWarning` that
says how many bytes were lost, instead of a later :meth:`!close` or the
finalizer trying to write it again. After a :exc:`KeyboardInterrupt`
interrupted a blocked write at exit, a second one is no longer needed, and a
broken pipe is no longer reported twice (:gh:`55589`). The
:class:`!TextIOWrapper` of :mod:`!_pyio` does the same. A private
:meth:`!_discard_writes` method of buffered objects drops the pending
writes.
