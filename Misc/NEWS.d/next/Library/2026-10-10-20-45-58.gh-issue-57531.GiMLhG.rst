:meth:`~io.BufferedReader.read1`, :meth:`~io.BufferedIOBase.readinto1` and
:meth:`~io.BufferedReader.peek` of :class:`io.BufferedReader` and
:class:`io.BufferedRandom` now return ``None`` when the raw stream would
block with nothing buffered, as :meth:`~io.BufferedIOBase.read` does,
instead of ``b""`` or ``0``, which mean end of file. The default
:meth:`io.BufferedIOBase.readinto` and :meth:`~io.BufferedIOBase.readinto1`
pass ``None`` on instead of raising :exc:`TypeError`. The
:class:`!TextIOWrapper` of :mod:`!_pyio` no longer decodes a partial
character as if at end of file when the stream would block.
