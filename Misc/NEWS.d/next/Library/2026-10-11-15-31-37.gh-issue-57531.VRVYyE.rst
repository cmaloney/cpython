On a non-blocking stream, :meth:`!read` with a size, :meth:`!readline` and
iteration of :class:`io.TextIOWrapper` now raise :exc:`BlockingIOError` when
no character is available, instead of returning ``""``, which means end of
file. :meth:`!readline` keeps a partial line for the next call when the
stream would block before the end of the line, unless it is longer than the
chunk size (8192 characters by default), and :meth:`!tell` stays at the
start of a kept line.
